FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for dealers, distributors and wholesale buyers.

Do you offer wholesale and dealer pricing?

Yes. Wholesale and dealer inquiries are welcome. Contact us with the model, estimated quantity and destination market for more information.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ can vary depending on the model, specifications and customization requirements. Contact us with the products you are interested in to discuss suitable options.

Can I customize the color or add my own brand?

Customization possibilities such as colors, branding and certain specifications can be discussed depending on the model and order requirements.

Do you ship internationally?

We welcome international business inquiries. Shipping options and arrangements depend on the destination, product type and order quantity.

How can I get a quotation?

Choose a product, tell us your country and estimated quantity, then contact us through WhatsApp. We will discuss your requirements and the next steps.
